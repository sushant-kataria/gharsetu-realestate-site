Problem

Launch teams still rely on scattered WhatsApp groups, PDFs, and one-off broker briefings.

Approach

Build one project system that agents can understand, share, and revisit without confusion.

Result

More consistent partner onboarding, fewer repeated questions, and better project recall in-market.

Launch checklist

  • Project overview, location logic, and investment angle
  • Inventory mix, unit sizes, and launch stage visibility
  • Brokerage slab and partner benefit articulation
  • Download-ready brochures and sales enablement assets
  • Direct CTA into the premium broker community

Suggested flow

  1. Broker lands on launch page from campaign or relationship outreach.
  2. Broker reviews deck, slab, unit mix, and nearby location advantages.
  3. Broker requests visit, brochure, or inventory conversation through one form.
