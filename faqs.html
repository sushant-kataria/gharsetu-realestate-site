Answers for brokers, builders, developers, and premium partners.

This FAQ page is split by audience so the questions are faster to scan on mobile and desktop.

General

What is GharSetu?

It is a modern multi-page proptech website for the Indian market covering agent discovery, listings, project pages, maps, content, and trust pages.

Is this a live marketplace?

This site includes a complete public page system for discovery, trust, pricing, support, and listings.

Can this support live CRM and lead flows later?

Yes. The page structure already separates discovery, trust, pricing, support, and detail-page templates for backend integration.

For agents

Does this help with builder floors?

Yes. Builder floors have their own public page and can also connect into agent matching and listing templates.

How is it different from large portals?

The positioning here is around curation, trust, and partner workflow rather than raw listing volume.

Can agents have a public profile?

Yes. The find-agent page uses reusable profile cards that can become full profile detail pages later.

For builders and developers

Can project-specific pages be created?

Yes. The sample project detail route is meant to be cloned into live project pages for launches and premium inventory.

Can brokerage slabs be displayed?

Yes. The Platinum Club and developer routes already reserve space for partner-specific benefits and slab framing.

Will this support brochures and floor plans?

Yes. Those can be attached to listing cards, project detail blocks, and launch pages.

Billing and support

Are prices shown in rupees?

Yes. This redesign switches pricing and copy fully into an India-first context.

Which support channels are assumed?

Email, phone, WhatsApp, and a sales contact form are already represented in the public pages.

Where are refund and legal details?

Separate privacy, terms, refund, and shipping pages are included in the website footer.
