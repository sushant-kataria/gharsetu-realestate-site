Sohna Road: Gurugram's mid-premium corridor with the widest buyer base.

Sectors 45 to 85 along Sohna Road serve the largest cross-section of buyers in Gurugram — from established gated communities to active new launches. BPTP, Smart World, Vatika, M3M, and Godrej all have significant presence here.

Sectors 45–85 ₹7,500–₹12,000/sq ft Family & ITES buyers Mid-premium
Segment

Mid to premium residential. Mix of established gated societies, township clusters, and active new launches.

Typical ticket size

₹1.8 Cr to ₹6 Cr. Entry-level 2 BHK starts at ₹1.8 Cr; luxury 4 BHK in Smart World Orchard or M3M Antalya reaches ₹7 Cr.

Best for

Mid-segment families, ITES professionals from Udyog Vihar and Sector 44 belt, Faridabad corridor buyers.

Key demand drivers

Good school access, proximity to HUDA City Centre Metro, well-established society clusters, and Faridabad connectivity.

Why buyers choose Sohna Road

  • Wider price range than any other Gurugram corridor — accessible from ₹1.8 Cr to ₹7 Cr+.
  • Established social infrastructure — schools, hospitals, malls — in Sectors 45–56.
  • Active rental market driven by ITES companies in Udyog Vihar and Sector 44.
  • Faridabad buyers treat Sohna Road as the upgrade destination — sustained demand from south.

Key talking points for agents

  • Sectors 49–51 have the most mature gated communities — good for ready-stock buyers.
  • Smart World Orchard (Sector 61) represents the new-luxury edge of this corridor.
  • Vatika City clusters (Sectors 49, 83) offer township living at a reasonable premium.
  • BPTP Park Serene (Sector 37D) serves price-sensitive buyers wanting gated community.
