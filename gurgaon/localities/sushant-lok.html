Sushant Lok: Gurugram's most established plotted and residential colony.

Sushant Lok 1 (Blocks A–F), SL-2, and SL-3 offer the rare combination of independent houses, plotted inventory, and resale apartments within easy reach of MG Road, HUDA City Centre Metro, and Cyber City.

Segment

Established mid-premium residential. Mix of independent houses, plotted plots, and resale apartment societies.

Typical ticket size

₹2.5 Cr to ₹7 Cr. Independent houses on larger plots push above ₹10 Cr in SL-1 Block A/B.

Best for

Families who want independent house character with city access. NRIs buying for parents. Buyers who value plotted ownership.

Metro connectivity

HUDA City Centre Metro is walkable from most SL-1 blocks. Direct Yellow Line access to Delhi central areas.

Why Sushant Lok buyers stay loyal

  • Independent house character unavailable in any DLF Phase at this price point.
  • HUDA City Centre Metro makes Delhi commute viable — important for dual-city professionals.
  • Mature social infrastructure: top schools, hospitals, and MGF Metropolitan Mall walkable.
  • Strong community identity — long-term residents maintain neighbourhood quality standards.

Block-level guidance for agents

  • SL-1 Block A/B — premium blocks, widest roads, highest independent house prices.
  • SL-1 Block D/E — more apartment societies, better price-point entry for buyers.
  • SL-2/SL-3 — newer development, slightly further from Metro, better plot availability.
  • Builder floors in SL-1 offer a mid-ticket option within a high-address colony.
