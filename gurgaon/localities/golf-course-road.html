Ultra-premium residential corridor

Golf Course Road, Gurugram: India's most prestigious residential address.

Sectors 42, 43, 53, and 54 host DLF's flagship luxury towers, the Golf & Country Club, and the highest per-sq-ft prices in the NCR. This is the corridor for HNI, C-suite, and returning NRI mandates.

Segment

Ultra-premium residential. Resale dominant with select new-build opportunities from DLF and Emaar.

Typical ticket size

₹5 Cr to ₹25 Cr+. Penthouses and bungalow-style DLF Magnolias units cross ₹30 Cr in active resale.

Best for

HNI professionals, returning NRIs, C-suite executives, and buyers who prioritise address over ticket size.

Live formats

Ultra-luxury high-rises (DLF, Emaar), independent DLF floors, penthouses, and branded residences.

Why this corridor commands a premium

  • DLF Golf & Country Club provides a genuine lifestyle anchor no other sector replicates.
  • Cyber City is under 10 minutes — the best work-home commute ratio in NCR for this price point.
  • Mature green cover, wide internal roads, and 24-hour security at every tower cluster.
  • Strong NRI demand ensures price stability and good resale liquidity at high ticket sizes.

What to cover in client conversations

  • Difference between DLF Crest (high-rise), Magnolias (bungalow towers), and Camellias (ultra-luxury).
  • Resale versus new-buy: pricing gaps, maintenance quality, and floor-plan vintage.
  • IGI Airport access via NH48 — a key decision factor for international travellers.
  • Golf Club membership availability — not automatic with every unit purchase.
