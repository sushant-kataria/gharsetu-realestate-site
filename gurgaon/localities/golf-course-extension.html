Golf Course Extension Road: Gurugram's fastest-growing premium corridor.

Sectors 56 to 68 have become the destination for upgrade buyers seeking golf-lifestyle living at a slightly more accessible ticket than Golf Course Road. M3M, DLF, and Mahindra anchor this belt.

Sectors 56–68 ₹11,000–₹17,000/sq ft Upgrade buyers Golf lifestyle
Segment

Premium to ultra-premium. Mix of ready-to-move resale and active under-construction primary launches.

Typical ticket size

₹3 Cr to ₹9 Cr for 2/3/4 BHK apartments. Penthouse and duplex inventory pushes above ₹12 Cr.

Best for

Upgrade families, golf-lifestyle seekers, investors targeting 3–5 year appreciation, and buyers comparing with Sohna Road.

Live formats

Premium high-rises (M3M, DLF Arbour), golf-facing towers, builder floors (Sector 57 belt), and plotted inventory.

Why buyers choose this corridor

  • Golf-facing unit availability at ₹3–7 Cr — not possible on Golf Course Road proper.
  • Southern Peripheral Road and NH48 connectivity ensures strong commute options.
  • School density (GD Goenka, DPS, Shri Ram) matches that of established DLF sectors.
  • High-end commercial hubs in Sectors 65–67 reduce weekend travel for everyday needs.

Key talking points for agents

  • M3M Golf Estate vs DLF Arbour — ready versus under-construction trade-off.
  • Sector 65–67 commercial catchment improving rapidly, driving rental demand.
  • Builder floors in Sector 57 offer lower entry tickets with same area access.
  • SPR connectivity to Cyber City is cleaner than Golf Course Road in peak hours.
