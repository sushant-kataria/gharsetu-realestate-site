Dwarka Expressway: Gurugram's highest-volume new launch corridor.

Sectors 76 to 115 span Gurugram's largest new-supply belt. Godrej Meridien, Sobha City, M3M Crown, Vatika, and Tata anchor this corridor. Strong IICC proximity and airport access make it a top investor and end-user destination.

Sectors 76–115 ₹8,500–₹14,000/sq ft Investor + end-user Primary launches
Segment

New-age primary launches + ready inventory. Largest volume of new supply in Gurugram. Mix of affordable-premium to luxury.

Typical ticket size

₹2.5 Cr to ₹8 Cr. Luxury projects by Godrej and Sobha push to ₹10 Cr+ for larger configurations.

Best for

Investors targeting 3–5 year appreciation, end-use buyers relocating from Delhi, and airport-proximity seekers.

Key demand drivers

India International Convention Centre (IICC), IGI Airport (15–20 min), NH48, Metro connectivity (planned extension).

Why buyers choose this corridor

  • IICC proximity makes this a commercial and hospitality hub in the making — rising land values likely.
  • IGI Airport in 15–20 minutes via Dwarka Expressway is the best airport-access corridor in Gurugram.
  • Multiple top-tier developer brands (Godrej, Tata, Sobha) reduce delivery risk for buyers.
  • Large township formats mean better clubhouses, amenities, and price stability post-possession.

Key talking points for agents

  • Sector 83 (Vatika) is the most mature sub-pocket — ready stock and active resale.
  • Sectors 106–112 are the current launch hotspot — highest new inventory concentration.
  • Metro extension will significantly improve daily commute to Cyber City.
  • Diplomatic Enclave proximity (Sector 77–83 belt) adds demand from expat community.

IICC corridor

Sectors 99–104

ATS Triumph (104), Hero Homes (104), Raheja Vanya (99). IICC-adjacent. Commercial upside baked in.
