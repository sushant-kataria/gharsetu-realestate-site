New Gurugram: Sectors 80–115, Gurugram's fastest-appreciating new supply zone.

Sectors 80 to 115 represent Gurugram's most dynamic new-development zone. Large-format townships by M3M, Signature Global, Hero Homes, Raheja, and others offer entry-level premium pricing with strong 3–5 year appreciation potential.

Segment

New supply — primary launches and early-stage projects. Township format dominates. Affordable-premium to mid-luxury range.

Typical ticket size

₹2 Cr to ₹6 Cr. Well-priced 3 BHK in a Signature Global or Hero Homes township starts at ₹2.2 Cr.

Best for

First-time Gurugram investors, buyers relocating from Delhi NCR periphery, end-users who want large amenity townships.

Infrastructure catalysts

NH48 expansion, Dwarka Expressway metro extension, and proximity to IICC drive future appreciation.

Investment case for New Gurugram

  • Lowest entry-point for a Gurugram address — real scarcity of quality affordable supply.
  • IICC becoming active will shift commercial gravity further south, lifting prices.
  • Large township format means better clubhouses, schools, and community quality versus standalone buildings.
  • Multiple brand-name developers (Tata, Godrej, Sobha) on Dwarka Expressway extension reduce delivery risk.

Risk factors to disclose

  • Possession timelines in under-construction projects may extend — verify RERA dates.
  • Social infrastructure is still developing — limited schools and hospitals in far sectors.
  • Rental yield is lower today; best suited to capital appreciation buyers, not rental income seekers.
  • Daily commute to Cyber City is 30–45 minutes — flag for buyers who need proximity.
