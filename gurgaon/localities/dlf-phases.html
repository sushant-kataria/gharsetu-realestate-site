DLF Phases 1–5: Gurugram's original ultra-premium township address.

From Phase 1 (Sector 26–28) to Phase 5 (Sector 43/53/54), DLF's township represents Gurugram's most sought-after residential address. Resale-dominant, mature infrastructure, and strong address premium make this a top choice for senior professionals and returning NRIs.

Segment

Established ultra-premium township. Resale dominates Phases 1–4. Phase 5 (GCR belt) has newer DLF products.

Typical ticket size

₹3 Cr to ₹30 Cr+. Phase 1 independent villas start at ₹10 Cr. Phase 5 luxury towers (Crest, Magnolias) push to ₹30 Cr+.

Best for

Senior professionals, returning NRIs, investors in an established market, and buyers who prioritise DLF address value.

Phase breakdown

Phase 1–3 (older residential, independent floors). Phase 4 (newer society clusters). Phase 5 (Golf Course Road — DLF's premium output).

What each DLF Phase offers buyers.

Phase 5 — Sector 43/53/54

Phase 5

DLF's Golf Course Road crown. The Crest, Magnolias, Camellias. Highest per-sq-ft DLF pricing. ₹8 Cr–₹35 Cr+.

Why DLF Phases hold value

  • Single-developer township means consistent maintenance, security, and community standards.
  • Proximity to Cyber City and Udyog Vihar — most strategic work-home location in Gurugram.
  • Strong NRI demand ensures dollar-linked price support even in flat market cycles.
  • Limited new supply within the phases — scarcity premium locked in.

What to cover in client conversations

  • Phase 1/2/3 versus Phase 5: older DLF character versus new luxury tower profile.
  • Independent villa/floor options in Phase 1 for buyers who want plotted-style ownership.
  • Maintenance charges vary widely — flag before shortlisting.
  • Golf Club membership: available for select DLF properties as a premium add-on.
